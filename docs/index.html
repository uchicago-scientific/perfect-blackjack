Perfect Blackjack

MPCS 56430 · Module 8
The rules
  • Cards 2 to 9 count their number; 10, J, Q and K count 10; an ace counts 11 or 1
  • An infinite deck: every card is drawn with replacement, so the chances never change
  • You see one dealer card, the upcard. Hit (take a card) or stand (stop); over 21 is a bust and loses at once
  • The dealer hits on 16 or less and stands on 17 or more, soft 17 included
  • Closer to 21 wins; a tie is a push and the bet comes back
  • Blackjack (21 in two cards) pays 3 to 2. A dealer blackjack beats every other hand; it shows when the hole card turns over
  • Only hit or stand: no doubling, splitting, surrender or insurance
